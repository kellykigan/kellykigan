## Research & Analytical Approach

My work focuses on using data and research methods to understand real-world development questions.

**Research question → Data preparation → Analysis → Visualisation → Interpretation → Evidence**

I aim to keep analytical work transparent, reproducible, and connected to the research question rather than treating statistical software or programming as an end in itself.

## AI & Research

I use AI as part of my research and analytical workflow, with an emphasis on **verification, critical evaluation, and responsible use**.

My Python work also explores how **AI agents can support research and data workflows**, including tasks such as data preparation, research assistance, analysis support, and synthesis.

The goal is to combine research judgement with technical tools while maintaining human review and validation of outputs.
